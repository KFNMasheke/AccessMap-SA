# Government facilities: discovery only

Status: **unvalidated idea; no implementation committed**.

Do not build an operational government edition until interviews and an inventory of existing systems establish a specific unmet need. This file is a discussion guide, not a product specification.

## Questions before proposing a product

- Who would use it: a facility manager, district planner, GIS analyst, outreach coordinator or another role?
- What recurring decision is difficult, and what happens when it is made poorly?
- What existing government, provincial, municipal or commercial system already supports that decision?
- Is the real problem missing software, poor data quality, inaccessible records, disconnected processes, staffing or budget?
- Could a QGIS template, data-quality check or export adapter solve it inside the existing system?
- Which organisation owns facility IDs, updates and correction approval? What can legally be published?
- Are distance, travel time, capacity, catchment population, service availability or accessibility actually the relevant measures?
- What evidence would show an improvement, and what would make us stop?

## Discovery output

Produce a short existing-tool inventory, interview notes with permission, one verified problem statement and a build/adapt/do-not-build recommendation. Default to improving an existing workflow when that meets the need. No account system, dashboards, resident data, integrations, procurement assumptions or government branding are included in v0.1.
