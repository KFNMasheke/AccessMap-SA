# Demo provenance

- Creator: Kaziwale Farai Masheke / AccessMap SA contributors.
- Generation date: 2026-10-19.
- Source: deterministic definitions in `scripts/build_demo.py`.
- Data licence: CC0-1.0 for original fictional facilities, representative points, grid cells and derived demonstration results.
- Spatial reference: EPSG:32735, WGS 84 / UTM zone 35S.
- Extent: a fictional layout placed around Gauteng; no claimed correspondence to actual facilities, settlements, property or administrative boundaries.
- Records: 320 synthetic cell-centre points; three fictional clinics; two fictional libraries. Two facilities intentionally fall outside the sample grid to demonstrate boundary effects.
- No real population, household records, facility contacts, roads, opening hours or verified accessibility data are included.
- Derived grid colours describe distance at cell centres only. Counts refer to points, never people.

The mobile pack derives latitude/longitude from the same synthetic coordinates for portability; its distance calculation still uses metre x/y coordinates. The source date describes generation, not field verification.
